WalkingCalories Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

WalkingCalories respects your privacy. The app is designed to provide walking calorie estimates without collecting your personal information.

Data Collection

WalkingCalories does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data or usage data.

Information You Enter

Information you enter into WalkingCalories, such as your steps, height, and weight, is used only on your device to calculate your estimated calories burned. This information is not transmitted to us or to third parties.

Third Parties

WalkingCalories does not sell or share your information with third parties and does not use your information for advertising or tracking.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the features or data practices of WalkingCalories change. Any updates will be posted on this page.
